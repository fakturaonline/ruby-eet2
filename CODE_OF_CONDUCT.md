# Code of conduct

We are committed to a professional, welcoming, and technically rigorous project.
Participants must communicate respectfully, give actionable feedback, protect
security-sensitive information, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
or disclosure of another person's private data.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior threatens contributors, users, the project, or coordinated vulnerability
handling. Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner; a permanent
maintainer contact will be published before the first public release.
